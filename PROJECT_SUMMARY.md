# EcoLoop Pack — project summary

EcoLoop Pack is a proposed managed reusable takeaway-container service for dense institutional foodservice sites. The project evaluates whether a local reuse system can combine container pooling, convenient returns, washing, inspection, redistribution and evidence reporting at a commercially credible level of participation and cost.

## Research-to-artefact logic

The research found that reuse performance cannot be represented by one return-rate assumption. Uptake, return, completed circulation, asset condition, washing, reverse logistics and commercial funding must be assessed separately. The artefact therefore operates as a pilot operations and feasibility system rather than as a generic QR demo.

## Main decision

The current evidence supports a controlled dense-site validation pilot, not a city-wide rollout, profitability claim or generic carbon-saving claim. Scale review should occur only after the eight evidence gates are complete and the observed performance passes the commercial and operational thresholds.

## Repository scope

This repository contains the artefact source, automated tests, architecture/testing notes, selected research evidence, financial planning evidence and academic posters. The assessed business-plan document and ethics/supervisor material remain in the university submission system.
