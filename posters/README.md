# Academic posters

Three academic poster outputs were prepared for the EcoLoop Pack project:

1. Academic research poster — research question, objectives, literature synthesis, methodology and conclusions.
2. Business and commercial feasibility poster — target buyer, value proposition, revenue logic, procurement evidence and implementation.
3. Artefact and pilot validation poster — lifecycle, KPIs, evidence gates, research-to-feature traceability and evaluation boundary.

The final poster PDFs are retained with the university submission package. This public repository records their scope without publishing duplicate assessed presentation files before submission.
